If you signed in to VibeFlow using Google or created account-linked data, you can use this page to request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

What Data May Be Deleted

  • Account identifiers such as your Google account email address and Firebase UID
  • Account-linked preferences or records associated with your VibeFlow account
  • Support or request records directly related to handling your account deletion request

Retention Information

We aim to process deletion requests in a reasonable time after verifying the request. Certain operational logs, security records, or backup data may be retained for up to 30 days before permanent deletion, where applicable.
